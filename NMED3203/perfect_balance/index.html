YOUR TASK

Observe. Act. Correct.

Try to prevent the ball from reaching either edge. Each button press changes the platform tilt by 4°. The ball keeps moving even after the platform returns to level.

  1. Watch the ball and the available feedback.
  2. Press Tilt Left or Tilt Right to change the slope.
  3. Use Level to make the platform flat.
  4. If the ball falls, select Reset attempt.
i

The ball starts with the same small rightward motion on every attempt. Tilt does not instantly cancel existing momentum.
